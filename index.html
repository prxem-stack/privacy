Privacy Policy — Prop Firm Calculator

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Prop Firm Calculator ("the App") is a calculator and journaling toolkit for proprietary-firm traders, developed by Prem Harsh ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App requires no account, no sign-up, and no internet connection to function.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Inputs you enter — selected account size and model, profitable-day entries, and trade-journal entries — are stored locally on your device only (local storage). This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising

If a version of the App displays ads, they are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Versions without ads collect nothing.

4. Analytics

The App contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from any user.

6. Disclaimer

Prop Firm Calculator is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any proprietary trading firm. All calculations are informational only and do not constitute financial, investment, or tax advice. Trading rules referenced in the App can change; the relevant firm's official dashboard and documentation are the sole authoritative source.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
