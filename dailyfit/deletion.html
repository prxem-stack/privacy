DailyFit / Data deletion

Your data.
Your choice.

DailyFit does not create an online account. Your local fitness records, optional downloaded model and submitted AI reports are managed separately.

Recommended order: delete any submitted AI reports first, remove the model if you want to, then delete your local profile or uninstall. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the private report-deletion keys saved on this phone.
1

Delete submitted AI reports

  1. Open Coach → Open offline AI → My AI reports.
  2. Choose Delete report using the trash icon beside each report receipt.
  3. Keep an internet connection and wait until that receipt disappears from the list. If deletion fails, the app asks you to check your connection and retry.

Successful deletion removes the matching report from the reporting service and its receipt from this phone. You do not need to submit another report. The service runs on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare; this website does not accept report submissions or deletion keys.

Reports contain only the question, answer and reason you explicitly chose to send, plus a random receipt ID and private deletion information. Reports are retained for 90 days and purged on the next reporting-service access unless you delete them earlier.

If your receipts have already been removed, contact premharsh.official@gmail.com and include the report receipt ID if you saved it. Do not email private deletion keys or medical records. Because reports have no account or device identifier attached, the developer may be unable to identify a particular report without its receipt ID.

2

Remove the downloaded AI model

  1. Open Coach → Open offline AI.
  2. Choose Remove downloaded model when the installed model is shown as ready and no download or answer is in progress.

This removes the installed optional model from the app. Core workouts and tracking remain available. Model removal does not delete your fitness profile, workout history or server reports.

3

Delete your local fitness records

  1. Open the You tab.
  2. Choose Delete profile & workout history.
  3. Confirm Delete in the confirmation dialog.

This erases the local profile, workout journal, readiness check-ins and water check-ins, then returns the app to profile setup. It does not delete the downloaded model or reports already submitted to the developer. Report receipts are preserved by this profile-reset action, but deleting reports first avoids needing to set up a profile again to reach the report screen.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes app-local data, including downloaded files and local report-deletion receipts. It does not automatically delete reports already submitted to the service. On iOS, local app data may also be included in a system backup according to your backup settings; manage those backups through your device or backup service.

?

Need help with deletion?

Email premharsh.official@gmail.com with your app version, device model and the step that did not work. Do not send private medical records.
