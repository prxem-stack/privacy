DailyFit / Data deletion · 1.6.0

Your data.
Your choice.

DailyFit can be used without an account. Local records, optional cloud accounts, model downloads and AI reports have separate deletion controls.

Updated 24 October 2026

Recommended order: delete submitted AI reports and any cloud data first, remove the model if you want to, then delete local fitness data or uninstall. Clearing app or browser data removes the private report-deletion receipts saved there.
1

Delete submitted AI reports

  1. In the same device or browser used to submit a report, open Profile → DailyFit Intelligence → Open offline AI → My AI reports.
  2. Choose Delete report using the trash icon beside its receipt.
  3. Keep an internet connection and wait until that receipt disappears. If deletion fails, check your connection and retry.

Successful deletion removes the matching server report and its local receipt. You do not need to submit another report. The service runs on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare; these public pages do not accept report submissions or deletion keys.

Reports contain the selected question, answer and category, plus a random receipt, timestamp and private deletion information. They are retained for 90 days and purged on the next reporting-service access unless deleted earlier. Saved fitness records are not attached separately, but the selected text can contain personal, health or fitness information.

If receipts were already removed, contact premharsh.official@gmail.com and include a receipt ID if you saved it. Do not email private deletion keys or medical records. Without an account or installation identifier attached to reports, the developer may be unable to identify a particular submission without its receipt ID.

2

Remove the downloaded AI model

  1. Open Profile → DailyFit Intelligence → Open offline AI.
  2. Choose Remove downloaded model when the model is ready and no download or answer is in progress.

Mobile Qwen uses an approximately 547 MB model; experimental browser Qwen uses approximately 512 MB plus runtime files. On mobile, removal deletes the installed model. In a browser, it stops the worker and removes cached Qwen model entries plus any legacy SmolLM2 model entries. Shared runtime files may remain in browser storage. If the experimental model cannot load or the removal control is unavailable, use browser site-data and cached-file controls after deleting any submitted reports. Browser storage may also be cleared or evicted by the browser.

Core workouts, programs and tracking remain available without the model. Model removal does not delete local fitness records, report receipts or server reports.

3

Delete your local fitness records

  1. Tap the Profile → Settings, labelled Your profile and settings.
  2. Choose Delete profile & workout history.
  3. Confirm Delete in Delete your local history?.

This erases the local profile and food exclusions, workout journal and unfinished draft, custom routines, exercises, favourites, weekly training days and progress photos, meal check-ins and notes, optional dated weight entries, readiness check-ins and water check-ins. Saved weekday equipment, training-cycle settings, automatic-backup configuration, queued posts and Health-export receipts are also removed. The device is signed out; workout reminders and rest alerts are cancelled. Once deletion succeeds, the app returns to profile setup.

The downloaded model and submitted reports are managed separately. Report receipts are preserved by this profile reset. Delete reports first to avoid needing to set up a profile again to reach them. Food and water screens have been removed; legacy records are deleted by the local reset. Optional measurements can be edited or deleted in Stats → Body → Your weight log. Exported workouts are separate copies: remove them from Apple Health if desired.

Uninstalling or clearing mobile app storage removes app-local data and report-deletion receipts, but does not automatically remove server reports or system backups. Android app backup is disabled. On iOS, manage any system backups through your device or backup service. Keychain sessions may survive uninstalling; use DailyFit sign out to clear saved access.

In a browser, clear site data for the website where you use the DailyFit app. This removes that app website's saved records and receipts; clearing data for these public support pages alone does not clear a different app website. Browser cache controls may be needed for remaining model/runtime files. Delete server reports before removing their local receipts.

4

Delete cloud and community data

  1. Open Community & cloud backup and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Delete just the cloud backup, remove individual posts, or use Delete cloud account.
  3. For account deletion, type your handle in the confirmation and wait for success. If storage deletion fails, retry; the app keeps the account available for retry.

Account deletion removes the account, backup, posts, selected community photos, likes, comments and replies, related activity notifications, follows, blocks, shared friend-comparison snapshots and rank contributions. Local fitness data is preserved. Rank contributions can also be removed from Ranks; the friend-comparison snapshot can be revoked from Account. Likes can be undone and your comments can be deleted from their post. Deleting a top-level comment removes its replies. Signing out or deleting the local profile does not delete cloud records. Use your saved recovery code to reset a forgotten password. Recovery revokes existing sessions. Keep the code private; do not email it. Sign out removes this device’s saved session, including secure mobile storage.

Unlinked photo uploads expire after one hour and are purged on later service access. When photo removal encounters an error, the image is inaccessible and removal is retried. Screenshots or copies saved by other members cannot be recalled. For assistance email support with your handle and a description, without sending passwords or medical records.
