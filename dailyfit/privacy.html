DailyFit is a local-first fitness application for adults aged 18 and over.

Information on your device

The application stores the profile details you choose to enter, workout records, readiness check-ins and water check-ins on your device. Optional weight and height fields are not required. No account is required. We do not upload your saved profile or workout records to a developer server or use advertising or analytics SDKs. Optional AI reports are described below.

Optional AI model

If you choose to download the optional model, the application contacts Hugging Face and its file delivery providers to download model files. These providers receive network information such as your IP address as part of serving the download. See Hugging Face's privacy policy. Your fitness profile and questions are not included in the download request. Inference runs locally on supported devices. Chat messages are held in memory for the current AI screen and are not intentionally retained after leaving it.

Optional AI content reports

When you select Report answer, the app shows exactly which question, answer and reason will be sent. Only after you choose Send report does it transmit that text over HTTPS to the DailyFit reporting service hosted using OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare. No saved profile, workout history, account identifier or device identifier is attached. Text you choose to report may contain fitness or personal information, so avoid including private medical details. Reports help the developer investigate unsafe, offensive or incorrect AI output; they are not used for advertising or analytics and are not public.

The service processes your network IP address for delivery and abuse prevention. It stores a rotating keyed hash for an hourly rate limit rather than the raw IP address. Infrastructure providers may process operational network logs under their own policies. Reports are retained for 90 days and purged on the next reporting-service access. The app stores a random receipt and private deletion key on this device. Use Offline AI → My AI reports → Delete report to remove a submission from the server. Keep app data until you have deleted any submissions you want removed; clearing data or uninstalling removes the local deletion key. Report review is restricted to the signed-in DailyFit developer.

Permissions and security

Internet access is used for model downloads and AI reports you explicitly send or delete. DailyFit does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location or health-sensor access. Android application backup is disabled. On iOS, local app data may be included in your device backup according to your system settings. Local information is protected by the operating system's application sandbox; use a secure device lock. No storage system is guaranteed completely secure.

Deletion and retention

Use You → Delete profile & workout history to erase local profile and activity records. Remove the downloaded model separately from Offline AI. Delete server AI reports separately using My AI reports before removing local app data. Uninstalling the application removes its app-local data. Records otherwise remain until you remove them or clear application storage.

Fitness scope

DailyFit offers general fitness information and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified professional for health restrictions. AI output may be inaccurate.

Website hosting

The public support, privacy and deletion pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

DailyFit adds no website analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or contact form to these pages. The optional in-app reporting service continues to run on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare, separately from this static website.

Contact

For assistance, contact DailyFit support at premharsh.official@gmail.com. Do not send private medical records. Include your app version, device model and a description of the issue.
