DailyFit is a local-first fitness application for adults aged 18 and over. No account, advertising or subscription is required.

Information on your device

DailyFit stores the profile details you choose to enter, food preferences and allergy exclusions, workout records and unfinished workout drafts, custom routines, meal check-ins and optional meal notes, optional dated body-weight entries, readiness check-ins and water check-ins. On mobile these records stay in the app's local storage; in a browser they stay in that browser's storage for the DailyFit app website. Weight and height are optional. We do not upload these saved records to a developer server or use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Profile weight is not automatically copied into weight history. Charts and journals use entries you actually save. Food ideas do not estimate your intake or create calorie targets. Optional AI content reports are handled separately below.

Optional AI model

Mobile AI uses the optional Qwen 2.5 0.5B model, approximately 547 MB. The experimental browser model is Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct, approximately 512 MB plus runtime files. Model files download from Hugging Face and its file delivery providers; the browser runtime also downloads from jsDelivr. Providers receive network information such as your IP address when delivering files. See Hugging Face's privacy policy. Your saved profile, workout history and questions are not included in file-download requests.

The AI feature is designed to process profile preferences, relevant workout context and questions locally on the mobile device or in the browser. No hosted chat API receives those prompts. The browser feature is experimental: successful generation and answer quality have not been verified for this release. Loading or generation can fail because of available memory, device or browser limits. Chat messages and reused daily answers are held in memory for the current AI screen and are not intentionally saved as chat history after leaving it.

Browser model files may remain in browser cache storage, and the browser may evict them. In the browser, Remove downloaded model deletes cached Qwen model entries and any legacy SmolLM2 model entries. Shared runtime files may remain. Use browser site-data and cached-file controls for remaining files. Deleting local fitness records does not automatically remove the model.

Optional AI content reports

When you select Report answer, the app shows the selected question, answer and reason. Only after you choose Send report does it transmit that text over HTTPS to the DailyFit reporting service hosted using OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare. Saved profile, routine, journal, meal and weight-history records are not attached as separate records. No account or installation identifier is attached. Selected text may nevertheless contain identity, health or fitness information you included or that appears in an answer; avoid reporting private medical details. Reports help investigate unsafe, offensive or incorrect output. They are not public and are not used for advertising or analytics.

The service processes your network IP address for delivery and abuse prevention. It stores a rotating keyed hash for an hourly rate limit rather than the raw IP address. Infrastructure providers may process operational network logs under their own policies. Reports are retained for 90 days and purged on the next reporting-service access unless deleted earlier. The app stores a random receipt and private deletion key on the device or browser used to submit the report. Use Coach → Open offline AI → My AI reports → Delete report to remove a submission. Keep its receipt until deletion succeeds; clearing app or browser data or uninstalling removes the local key. Review is restricted to the signed-in DailyFit developer.

Exercise videos

The exercise library includes 27 adapted demonstrations by Goulart from the wger catalogue, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Mobile builds include the video and preview assets. Browser playback loads assets from the website hosting the DailyFit app, so the first load needs internet and hosting providers may process ordinary delivery logs. Playback does not automatically contact wger. Opening a source or licence link contacts the corresponding website. Some exercises have written cues only.

Permissions and security

Internet is used to load the browser app and its assets, download optional model/runtime files, and send or delete AI reports you explicitly choose. DailyFit does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location or health-sensor access. Android application backup is disabled. On iOS, app data may be included in device backups according to your system settings. Mobile records are protected by the operating system's app sandbox; browser records use the browser's storage protections. Use a secure device lock and browser profile. No storage system is guaranteed completely secure.

Deletion and retention

Open the profile icon at the top of the app, choose Delete profile & workout history, then confirm Delete. This removes the local profile and exclusions, workout history and draft, custom routines, meal records and notes, optional weight history, readiness and water records. Remove the downloaded model and server AI reports separately in Offline AI. The profile-reset action preserves report receipts so you can still delete reports.

Uninstalling or clearing mobile app storage removes app-local data but does not automatically delete submitted reports or system backups. In a browser, clear site data for the website where you use the DailyFit app to remove its saved records and receipts; clearing these public support pages alone does not erase data saved by a different app website. Browser cache settings may be needed for remaining model/runtime files. Delete reports before clearing any receipts. Other local records remain until you remove them or clear storage; your browser or system may also clear local storage.

Fitness scope

DailyFit offers general fitness and food ideas and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Meal suggestions are not a prescribed diet or an allergy-safety guarantee; check ingredients, labels and preparation. Consult a qualified professional for medical restrictions or individual dietary needs. AI output may be inaccurate.

Website hosting

These public support, privacy and deletion pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

DailyFit adds no website analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or contact form to these public pages. The optional reporting service continues to run on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare, separately from this static website. The DailyFit browser app may be hosted separately; its hosting provider processes ordinary requests needed to deliver the app and assets.
