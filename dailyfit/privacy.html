Last updated: 24 October 2026 · DailyFit 1.7.0

DailyFit is a local-first fitness application for adults aged 18 and over. No account is required for local training. Optional accounts provide reviewed backups, optional private record sync, and community features. No advertising or subscription is required.

Information on your device

DailyFit stores the profile details you enter, workout records and unfinished drafts, custom routines and exercises, favourites, weekly training days, equipment and training-cycle choices, readiness check-ins, optional body-weight entries, and any retained meal or water records. Older food and water screens are no longer in the main navigation; their saved records remain available to deletion and supported export. Local photos, body-scan views, pose results, tape-measure estimates and scan history remain in app storage. On mobile this is the operating system's app storage; browser records use the app website's local storage.

Training needs no account. Portable fitness records stay local unless you upload a reviewed backup, enable automatic backup, or enable private two-way sync. Saved weight measurements, including weights you imported from Apple Health, are portable and can be included in those opted-in transfers. Photos, body scans and their measurements are excluded. Community sharing requires a separate choice. DailyFit has no advertisements or product analytics. Optional Google SDK diagnostics are described under Body scans and Permissions below.

Weight and height are optional. Profile weight is not automatically copied to the weight journal. Charts use entries you save. Food ideas do not measure intake or create calorie targets. AI reports are handled separately.

Optional cloud account and backups

An optional account uses a pseudonymous handle and password; no email or real name is required. The DailyFit service on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare stores your handle, salted PBKDF2 password hash and hashes of expiring session tokens. Sessions last up to seven days. Mobile sign-in tokens use iOS Keychain or Android secure storage; browser tokens remain in memory. iOS Keychain can survive uninstalling, so sign out to revoke saved access. A private, single-use recovery code can reset the password and revoke sessions. Only its hash is kept on the service. Losing both password and recovery code may prevent recovery.

Reviewed and automatic backups: Review & upload backup shows the data before sending it. Portable records include your profile, workouts, routines, schedules, weekday equipment, training-cycle settings, saved body-weight measurements, legacy meal records, custom exercises, favourites and weight-unit choice. Automatic backup is off by default and requires review and consent. While the app is open it checks approximately every two minutes and on resume; it uploads changes to the latest private backup and pauses when the remote revision changes. Restore previews a merge. Backups are private but not end-to-end encrypted.

Optional private two-way sync: This separate opt-in feature synchronizes the supported portable records between devices signed in to your account. It checks about every 30 seconds while the app is open and when you return. Changes to different records can merge; conflicting changes to the same record require your choice before they are resolved. Record deletions create deletion markers so an older device cannot silently restore removed records. On iPhone and Android, the existing app process may be given a short opportunity to finish a sync after you leave; the operating system may stop it. This is not continuous synchronization while the app is closed, and offline changes wait for a later successful connection.

Enabling sync sends supported saved measurements, including imported Apple Health weights, along with the other previewed records. Scan photos, pose results, tape estimates and scan history, model files, AI conversations, account credentials, report-deletion receipts, Health export receipts and OS permissions are not synchronized or included in backups. Sync is private to your account and not end-to-end encrypted; it does not publish community content. Disabling sync stops future synchronization but does not erase existing cloud records. Clear the synchronized cloud records separately, or delete the cloud account to remove its sync state. Manual backup is managed separately. Local JSON export uses the clipboard; your operating system may make clipboard content available to other apps or devices.

Optional community and comparisons

Accounts start private. Enabling community visibility makes your handle discoverable to other signed-in accounts. Each post requires a separate sharing choice. You can choose a photo specifically for a community post; progress photos are never attached automatically. Selected photos are uploaded only when you publish, limited to JPEG images up to 700 KB and 2560 pixels per dimension. The service strips EXIF and other application metadata, including location metadata. Posts and photos are accessible only to signed-in accounts allowed to view the post; someone who has already viewed content may retain a copy.

Turning off community visibility hides your posts from other accounts. You can follow, unfollow, block, report or delete posts. Likes, comments and replies are stored with your account and the related post. In-app activity notifications store the actor, recipient, related post or comment, timestamp and read time. Turning visibility off also hides your social interactions from other accounts; blocking applies to posts, profiles, comments, comparison snapshots and activity. You may mention visible accounts you follow in a post or comment. Mentions create in-app activity for eligible recipients. In-app Activity remains available without push. Optional remote community alerts are implemented for APNs on iPhone and Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, but are currently not activated because DailyFit provider configuration is incomplete. The app shows this state and does not claim delivery. When configured, enabling alerts requires your account, selected activity categories and OS permission. The service stores an encrypted device registration token, its hash, session binding, chosen categories and registration times. A provider receives the device token and a generic DailyFit activity message with an opaque binding; no handle, post/comment text, body scan, health data or account ID is in the payload. Opening the notification checks the active account binding and then loads Activity through authenticated requests. Sign out, password recovery or account deletion revokes the relevant registrations; disabling alerts removes this device's registration. Delivery is best effort and device settings can suppress it. An alert already in transit cannot always be recalled. Browser push is not implemented. Explicitly submitted posts that cannot upload are kept in an account-scoped local retry queue with their caption and any selected JPEG. The queue is shown in Community and requires retry or deletion; it does not share another account’s queued content. A report sends the post or comment identifier and reason to the developer, who can review the reported text/photo or comment and hide it. Do not publish harassment, hateful or sexual content, dangerous advice, spam, or others’ private information. Contact support for safety concerns.

Friend comparisons require a separate explicit choice to publish a previewed snapshot of logged session and working-set totals and selected estimated one-rep maximum lifts in kilograms. This snapshot is self-reported and visible only through your community profile to permitted signed-in members. Backups never automatically populate public statistics. You can revoke the snapshot in Account; hiding your profile also hides it. It does not share bodyweight or full workout history.

Strength comparisons are separately optional. After review, the service stores a movement identifier, chosen experience group and calculated bodyweight-adjusted strength index under your account. It does not receive your raw bodyweight or complete workout through this comparison feature. Results use self-reported contributions from the last year and show a percentile only when at least 30 distinct accounts contributed to that movement/group. Individual contribution records and handles are not listed in comparison results. Movement percentiles can be averaged into muscle and group scores; an overall score requires at least 10 eligible movements. These aggregate scores are not population percentiles. Each qualifying movement still needs at least 30 contributors. This is DailyFit’s documented index, not Symmetry’s algorithm or a verified measure of fitness. Remove contributions from the comparison screen or by deleting your account. The hypothetical rank calculator sends the chosen exercise, experience group and calculated test index when you request a comparison; this preview does not save a contribution. Post-workout rank updates require a separate review and share action.

Private body scans and tape estimates

Body scan lets you explicitly take or choose front, side and back photos. Camera permission is requested when you choose the camera; the system picker limits access to selected photos. Scan images and results are stored locally only after you save a private scan. They are not uploaded for analysis, synchronized, included in portable backups or automatically shared in Community. You can delete an individual scan and its saved views from scan history. Copy report text copies only the displayed text to the clipboard, not its photos.

On supported iPhones, Apple Vision performs pose detection on device. Its 2D joints and optional estimated 3D pose use the framework's reference scale; they do not provide an accurate body surface, body dimensions or a clinical scan. Android's bundled Google ML Kit detects 2D pose points when you explicitly request body-scan analysis. Community photo selection does not request pose detection. Front/side/back labels are your choices; visible angles and framing depend on camera perspective and detection quality.

Google states that ML Kit input images and detection outputs are processed on device and are not sent to Google servers. Its SDK can send device and app information, per-installation identifiers, feature/configuration information, performance metrics and error events for diagnostics and usage analytics. Pose detection also uses Firebase remote configuration and installation diagnostics. This SDK information is distinct from your photos and pose results. See ML Kit privacy information and Android SDK data disclosures.

The optional adult tape-measure body-fat estimate uses the height and circumference measurements you enter into the selected published equation. It does not infer a percentage from a photo. Inputs and estimates stay in the saved local scan, excluded from portable sync/backup. The estimate is approximate, intended only for adults who are not pregnant, and can differ substantially from a clinical assessment. Pose detection and tape estimates do not diagnose conditions, assess genetics or attractiveness, or replace a qualified professional.

Optional AI model

Mobile AI uses the optional Qwen 2.5 0.5B model, approximately 547 MB. The experimental browser model is Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct, approximately 512 MB plus runtime files. Model files download from Hugging Face and its file delivery providers; the browser runtime also downloads from jsDelivr. Providers receive network information such as your IP address when delivering files. See Hugging Face's privacy policy. Your saved profile, workout history and questions are not included in file-download requests.

The AI feature is designed to process profile preferences, relevant workout context and questions locally on the mobile device or in the browser. No hosted chat API receives those prompts. The browser feature is experimental: answers can depart from the supplied workout plan, or be incomplete. Local checks may withhold outputs, but do not guarantee correctness. Core workout plans and tracking remain available without the model. Loading or generation can fail because of available memory, device or browser limits. Chat messages and reused daily answers are held in memory for the current AI screen and are not intentionally saved as chat history after leaving it.

Browser model files may remain in browser cache storage, and the browser may evict them. In the browser, Remove downloaded model deletes cached Qwen model entries and any legacy SmolLM2 model entries. Shared runtime files may remain. Use browser site-data and cached-file controls for remaining files. Deleting local fitness records does not automatically remove the model.

Optional AI content reports

When you select Report answer, the app shows the selected question, answer and reason. Only after you choose Send report does it transmit that text over HTTPS to the DailyFit reporting service hosted using OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare. Saved profile, routine, journal, meal and weight-history records are not attached as separate records. No account or installation identifier is attached. Selected text may nevertheless contain identity, health or fitness information you included or that appears in an answer; avoid reporting private medical details. Reports help investigate unsafe, offensive or incorrect output. They are not public and are not used for advertising or analytics.

The service processes your network IP address for delivery and abuse prevention. It stores a rotating keyed hash for an hourly rate limit rather than the raw IP address. Infrastructure providers may process operational network logs under their own policies. Reports are retained for 90 days and purged on the next reporting-service access unless deleted earlier. The app stores a random receipt and private deletion key on the device or browser used to submit the report. Use Profile → DailyFit Intelligence → Open offline AI → My AI reports → Delete report to remove a submission. Keep its receipt until deletion succeeds; clearing app or browser data or uninstalling removes the local key. Review is restricted to the signed-in DailyFit developer.

Exercise videos

The library combines bundled technique clips by Goulart from the wger catalogue, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, with selected public-domain exercise demonstrations from DVIDS. Individual source credits and licence information are shown with each demonstration. The Goulart clips have been resized, encoded and muted; adapted clips and preview frames retain the same licence. This does not imply endorsement by their creators.

Bundled mobile videos can play offline. DVIDS demonstrations stream from their remote video host when you choose playback, which sends ordinary connection information such as your IP address, requested file and client information to that host. Your fitness records, photos and account credentials are not included. See DVIDS privacy information. Opening source or licence links also contacts those websites.

Optional explicit offline downloads save selected remote videos in an app cache limited to 256 MB. A saved copy can play without internet until removed or evicted; an uncached remote video needs a connection. Offline video downloads are supported in the native iPhone and Android apps. Browser playback streams remote videos and does not provide this native download cache. Remove cached downloads in Library → Exercise library → Manage downloaded videos → Clear downloads, or remove a saved video from its demonstration screen. Clearing video cache does not delete workout records or the separately downloaded AI model.

For DVIDS content, see copyright and use information. The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement.

Permissions and security

Internet access supports model and video downloads, video streaming, AI reports and optional cloud/community features. DailyFit does not request contacts, microphone or location access. Camera access is requested when you choose to capture a body-scan photo; system photo pickers access images you select. Notification permission supports separately opted-in local reminders, rest alerts and, when configured, remote community alerts. Local alerts use OS scheduling; device settings, battery restrictions and Focus can delay or silence them. Configured Firebase messaging uses installation/device registration information to deliver Android notifications; it does not receive your workout database or scan images.

On iPhone, Apple Health weight read and workout write permissions are separate. You review weight imports; imported weight measurements are saved locally and may later be uploaded through reviewed backup, automatic backup or private sync that you enabled. Workout export previews selected locally recorded strength sessions and writes their recorded timing and active duration, without estimated calories or sensor readings. Imported workout history is excluded from export. Local receipts and stable workout IDs reduce duplicate writes. Exported copies stay in Apple Health until removed there. Health permissions and export receipts are not portable.

Android app backup is disabled. iOS device backups may include local app data according to your system settings. Local storage relies on the operating system's app sandbox; use a secure device lock. Cloud transport uses HTTPS. Device registration tokens are encrypted on the service, but fitness backups and sync are not end-to-end encrypted. No storage or delivery system is guaranteed completely secure. Live Activities can show the current exercise and rest timer on the iPhone lock screen; device settings control this. Browser app/runtime hosting providers process requests needed to deliver those files.

Deletion and retention

Profile → Settings → Delete profile & workout history removes local fitness records, progress photos, saved body scans and tape estimates, queued posts, backup/sync choices, device-notification preferences and Health export receipts. The device is signed out, remote registration is revoked where reachable, and local workout reminders/rest alerts are cancelled. Report-deletion receipts and downloaded AI models have separate controls. Exported Apple Health workouts, exported files, clipboard copies and system backups are managed separately.

Cloud controls separately erase the latest backup, clear synchronized cloud records, delete individual community posts or delete the entire cloud account. Clearing synchronized records retains deletion markers to protect other devices from resurrecting removed records. Deleting the account removes its sync records and markers, backup, recovery/session data, device registrations, posts, selected community photos, likes, comments/replies, related Activity, follows/blocks, shared comparison snapshots and rank contributions. Local data is preserved unless you also choose local deletion. Disabling sync or signing out does not by itself delete those cloud records. Copies already retained by other people cannot be recalled.

Unlinked community-photo uploads expire after one hour and are purged on later service access. Failed storage removal makes an image inaccessible and is retried; account deletion is not reported complete until required storage removal succeeds. Comparison scores stop contributing after one year. Other records remain until deleted. Submitted AI reports have their separate 90-day retention and receipt-based deletion process. See the steps below before clearing receipts.

Uninstalling or clearing browser/app data removes app-local data and cached downloads, but does not delete server records or OS backups. iOS Keychain entries may survive uninstalling; sign out to revoke the session. Clear the DailyFit app website's data rather than only this support website. Remove AI models and cached videos with their own controls when you want to free downloads without erasing workouts.

Fitness scope

DailyFit offers general fitness and food ideas and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Meal suggestions are not a prescribed diet or an allergy-safety guarantee; check ingredients, labels and preparation. Consult a qualified professional for medical restrictions or individual dietary needs. AI output may be inaccurate.

Website hosting

These public support, privacy and deletion pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

DailyFit adds no website analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or contact form to these public pages. The optional reporting service continues to run on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare, separately from this static website. The DailyFit browser app may be hosted separately; its hosting provider processes ordinary requests needed to deliver the app and assets.
