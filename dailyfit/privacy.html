Last updated: 24 October 2026 · DailyFit 1.5.0

DailyFit is a local-first fitness application for adults aged 18 and over. No account is required for local training. Optional accounts provide manual backup and community features. No advertising or subscription is required.

Information on your device

DailyFit stores the profile details you choose to enter, legacy food preferences and allergy exclusions, workout records and unfinished workout drafts, custom routines, exercise entries, favourites and saved weekly training days, legacy meal check-ins and optional meal notes, optional dated body-weight entries, readiness check-ins and legacy water check-ins. Food and water screens are no longer part of the 1.5 navigation; existing records remain exportable and deletable. On mobile these records stay in the app's local storage; in a browser they stay in that browser's storage for the DailyFit app website. Weight and height are optional. Saved records are not uploaded automatically. Optional cloud backups and sharing require explicit actions. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Profile weight is not automatically copied into weight history. Charts and journals use entries you actually save. Food ideas do not estimate your intake or create calorie targets. Optional AI content reports are handled separately below.

Optional cloud account and backups

You can choose a pseudonymous handle and password. The DailyFit service, hosted using OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare, stores your handle, a randomly salted PBKDF2 password hash and hashes of expiring session tokens. No email or real name is required. Sessions expire after seven days and the app keeps its token only in memory; restarting requires sign-in. Password recovery is not currently available.

Choosing Review & upload backup shows the exact data before sending it. Only after confirmation does the app upload your profile, workout history, routines, schedule, saved measurements, meal records, custom exercises, favorites and weight-unit preference. Backup is manual; no automatic background sync occurs. The latest server backup replaces the previous backup, with a revision check to prevent stale updates. Backups are private to your account but are not end-to-end encrypted. AI models, AI conversations, report deletion receipts, credentials, progress photos and OS permissions are excluded. Restore previews a merge that keeps existing local records on conflicts. Local JSON export uses the clipboard, which may be accessible to other apps or devices according to your operating-system settings.

Optional community and comparisons

Accounts start private. Enabling community visibility makes your handle discoverable to other signed-in accounts. Each post requires a separate sharing choice. You can choose a photo specifically for a community post; progress photos are never attached automatically. Selected photos are uploaded only when you publish, limited to JPEG images up to 700 KB and 2560 pixels per dimension. The service strips EXIF and other application metadata, including location metadata. Posts and photos are accessible only to signed-in accounts allowed to view the post; someone who has already viewed content may retain a copy.

Turning off community visibility hides your posts from other accounts. You can follow, unfollow, block, report or delete posts. Likes, comments and replies are stored with your account and the related post. In-app activity notifications store the actor, recipient, related post or comment, timestamp and read time. Turning visibility off also hides your social interactions from other accounts; blocking applies to posts, profiles, comments, comparison snapshots and activity. Notifications appear in the app; social push notifications are not enabled. A report sends the post or comment identifier and reason to the developer, who can review the reported text/photo or comment and hide it. Do not publish harassment, hateful or sexual content, dangerous advice, spam, or others’ private information. Contact support for safety concerns.

Friend comparisons require a separate explicit choice to publish a previewed snapshot of logged session and working-set totals and selected estimated one-rep maximum lifts in kilograms. This snapshot is self-reported and visible only through your community profile to permitted signed-in members. Backups never automatically populate public statistics. You can revoke the snapshot in Account; hiding your profile also hides it. It does not share bodyweight or full workout history.

Strength comparisons are separately optional. After review, the service stores a movement identifier, chosen experience group and calculated bodyweight-adjusted strength index under your account. It does not receive your raw bodyweight or complete workout through this comparison feature. Results use self-reported contributions from the last year and show a percentile only when at least 30 distinct accounts contributed to that movement/group. Individual contribution records and handles are not listed in comparison results. Movement percentiles can be averaged into muscle and group scores; an overall score requires at least 10 eligible movements. These aggregate scores are not population percentiles. Each qualifying movement still needs at least 30 contributors. This is DailyFit’s documented index, not Symmetry’s algorithm or a verified measure of fitness. Remove contributions from the comparison screen or by deleting your account.

Optional AI model

Mobile AI uses the optional Qwen 2.5 0.5B model, approximately 547 MB. The experimental browser model is Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct, approximately 512 MB plus runtime files. Model files download from Hugging Face and its file delivery providers; the browser runtime also downloads from jsDelivr. Providers receive network information such as your IP address when delivering files. See Hugging Face's privacy policy. Your saved profile, workout history and questions are not included in file-download requests.

The AI feature is designed to process profile preferences, relevant workout context and questions locally on the mobile device or in the browser. No hosted chat API receives those prompts. The browser feature is experimental: answers can depart from the supplied workout plan, or be incomplete. Local checks may withhold outputs, but do not guarantee correctness. Core workout plans and tracking remain available without the model. Loading or generation can fail because of available memory, device or browser limits. Chat messages and reused daily answers are held in memory for the current AI screen and are not intentionally saved as chat history after leaving it.

Browser model files may remain in browser cache storage, and the browser may evict them. In the browser, Remove downloaded model deletes cached Qwen model entries and any legacy SmolLM2 model entries. Shared runtime files may remain. Use browser site-data and cached-file controls for remaining files. Deleting local fitness records does not automatically remove the model.

Optional AI content reports

When you select Report answer, the app shows the selected question, answer and reason. Only after you choose Send report does it transmit that text over HTTPS to the DailyFit reporting service hosted using OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare. Saved profile, routine, journal, meal and weight-history records are not attached as separate records. No account or installation identifier is attached. Selected text may nevertheless contain identity, health or fitness information you included or that appears in an answer; avoid reporting private medical details. Reports help investigate unsafe, offensive or incorrect output. They are not public and are not used for advertising or analytics.

The service processes your network IP address for delivery and abuse prevention. It stores a rotating keyed hash for an hourly rate limit rather than the raw IP address. Infrastructure providers may process operational network logs under their own policies. Reports are retained for 90 days and purged on the next reporting-service access unless deleted earlier. The app stores a random receipt and private deletion key on the device or browser used to submit the report. Use Profile → DailyFit Intelligence → Open offline AI → My AI reports → Delete report to remove a submission. Keep its receipt until deletion succeeds; clearing app or browser data or uninstalling removes the local key. Review is restricted to the signed-in DailyFit developer.

Exercise videos

The exercise library includes adapted technique video clips by Goulart from the wger catalogue, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Mobile builds include the video and preview assets. Browser playback loads assets from the website hosting the DailyFit app, so the first load needs internet and hosting providers may process ordinary delivery logs. Playback does not automatically contact wger. Opening a source or licence link contacts the corresponding website. Some exercises have multiple demonstrated views; exercises without videos have written cues. Catalogue attributions and licence links are available with each exercise.

Permissions and security

Internet access is used for model downloads, explicit AI reports and optional cloud/community actions. DailyFit does not request contacts, camera, microphone or location access. Optional Android and iPhone features use the system photo picker for images you select and notification permission for reminders. On iPhone, optional read-only Apple Health weight access is requested separately. Imported weight measurements stay local unless you later explicitly include them in a cloud backup. Progress photos stay local and are excluded from backups; on iPhone, optional on-device pose landmarks cannot measure body fat, attractiveness, genetics or strength and are not a diagnosis. These OS permissions stay on the device and are not included in backups. Android application backup is disabled. On iOS, local app data may be included in your device backup according to your system settings. Local information is protected by the operating system's application sandbox; use a secure device lock. No storage system is guaranteed completely secure.

Browser use loads app assets from its hosting provider. Browser runtime files can download from jsDelivr. Local browser records remain in that browser’s site storage. Live Activities can show your current exercise and rest timer on the iPhone lock screen. You can disable these in device settings.

Deletion and retention

Use Profile → Settings → Delete profile & workout history to erase local profile and activity records. Remove the downloaded model separately from Offline AI. Delete server AI reports separately using My AI reports before removing local app data. Cloud & community lets you delete the cloud backup alone or permanently delete your cloud account, posts, photos, likes, comments and replies, related notifications, follows, shared comparison snapshots and rank contributions. Local app deletion/uninstall does not delete cloud records. Cloud records otherwise remain until deleted; scores stop contributing to comparisons after one year. Unlinked uploaded photos expire after one hour and are purged on the next cloud-service access. If photo deletion encounters a storage error, the object is made inaccessible and deletion is retried on later service access; the app does not claim account deletion succeeded until object removal completes. Uninstalling the application removes its app-local data. Records otherwise remain until you remove them or clear application storage.

Clear browser site data for the app website to remove browser-local records. Removing support-page data alone does not clear a separate app website. Remove downloaded models separately and delete AI reports before losing their private receipts. Local export copies records to the clipboard; clear it when finished. Local deletion also removes progress photos and turns off DailyFit workout reminders.

Fitness scope

DailyFit offers general fitness and food ideas and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Meal suggestions are not a prescribed diet or an allergy-safety guarantee; check ingredients, labels and preparation. Consult a qualified professional for medical restrictions or individual dietary needs. AI output may be inaccurate.

Website hosting

These public support, privacy and deletion pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

DailyFit adds no website analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or contact form to these public pages. The optional reporting service continues to run on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare, separately from this static website. The DailyFit browser app may be hosted separately; its hosting provider processes ordinary requests needed to deliver the app and assets.
