A few simple steps to get started with the app.

01

Make it yours

Create your adult profile, then select your goal and equipment. No online account is created.

02

Check in first

Complete the readiness check before training. Follow the workout at a comfortable pace and stop if an exercise causes pain.

03

Keep your own record

Track the sets, reps and weights you complete, then revisit your own workout journal.

Optional offline AI

A little help, on your phone.

Core workouts do not need a model download. The optional Qwen model is approximately 547 MB and requires compatible hardware.

Use Wi-Fi and keep at least 1.5 GB of storage free. If the model cannot run on your phone, Quick Coach and workout tracking remain available.
