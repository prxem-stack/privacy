A few simple steps to get started with the app.

01

Make it yours

Create your adult profile, then select your goal, equipment, experience and weekly training days. No account is needed for local training; cloud and community accounts are optional.

02

Check in first

Complete the readiness check before training. Follow the workout at a comfortable pace and stop if an exercise causes pain.

03

Keep your own record

Log sets, reps, weights, duration, distance and effort. Build supersets and a weekly routine. Record optional body-weight measurements, and revisit your own history.

Optional local AI

A little help, where you train.

Core workouts and programs do not need a model download. Mobile AI uses Qwen 2.5 0.5B, about 547 MB. The experimental browser model is Qwen2.5 0.5B, about 512 MB plus runtime files.

Models download from Hugging Face; the browser runtime also downloads from jsDelivr. The AI is designed to generate answers locally. Browser AI is experimental: answers can depart from the supplied workout plan, or be incomplete. Local checks may withhold outputs, but do not guarantee correctness. Loading or generation may fail because of device memory or browser limits. For mobile AI, use Wi-Fi and keep at least 1.5 GB free. Training guidance and tracking remain available without AI.

A little guidance for each day.

Choose the parts that fit your routine.

Train with clear cues

Explore exercises, build a custom routine and record completed sets. The library includes searchable exercises, bundled licensed technique clips and selected public-domain demonstration videos, with multiple views for selected movements. Each demonstration includes its source and licence information.

Bundled mobile clips work offline. Remote demonstrations stream when played; you can explicitly save selected videos in a 256 MB offline cache. Browser remote playback streams videos; native offline downloads are available on iPhone and Android.

Find your next program

Use Library to preview free programs, adopt a weekly plan or edit your own routines. Set Home, Gym or Travel equipment for each weekday, review substitutions, and use an optional eight-week training cycle while retaining your records.

See your own progress

Weight logging and local progress photos are optional. Charts use saved measurements; muscle sets and strength estimates use your actual workout records. Optional community comparisons need at least 30 contributors. Private body scans offer front/side/back photos, local pose previews and optional tape-measure estimates. Photos are not used to guess body fat, genetics or attractiveness. DailyFit does not invent intake, calories or progress.

Private records across your devices

Opt-in two-way sync checks supported records every 30 seconds while open and on resume. Separate edits merge; conflicting edits require your choice. Deletion markers protect removed records. Brief background finishing depends on the phone; there is no guarantee of sync while closed. Saved weights, including reviewed Health imports, can sync. Scan photos, tape estimates and pose results stay local.

Community activity

Follow people, comment, reply, mention and review your Activity. Optional generic APNs/FCM alerts have been implemented, but remote delivery is not activated until DailyFit provider setup is complete. The app displays that limitation. No push delivery or public store availability is promised by this support page.
