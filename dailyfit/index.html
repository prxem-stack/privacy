A few simple steps to get started with the app.

01

Make it yours

Create your adult profile, then select your goal, equipment, experience and weekly training days. No account is needed for local training; cloud and community accounts are optional.

02

Check in first

Complete the readiness check before training. Follow the workout at a comfortable pace and stop if an exercise causes pain.

03

Keep your own record

Log sets, reps, weights, duration, distance and effort. Build supersets and a weekly routine. Record meals and optional body-weight measurements, and revisit your own history.

Optional local AI

A little help, where you train.

Core workouts and programs do not need a model download. Mobile AI uses Qwen 2.5 0.5B, about 547 MB. The experimental browser model is Qwen2.5 0.5B, about 512 MB plus runtime files.

Models download from Hugging Face; the browser runtime also downloads from jsDelivr. The AI is designed to generate answers locally. Browser AI is experimental: answers can depart from the supplied workout plan, or be incomplete. Local checks may withhold outputs, but do not guarantee correctness. Loading or generation may fail because of device memory or browser limits. For mobile AI, use Wi-Fi and keep at least 1.5 GB free. Training guidance and tracking remain available without AI.

Keep control of your data.

Your local records, downloaded model, submitted AI reports and optional cloud account are managed separately.

A little guidance for each day.

Choose the parts that fit your routine.

Train with clear cues

Explore exercises, build a custom routine and record completed sets. The library includes searchable exercises and licensed technique videos, with multiple views for selected movements. Some exercises use written cues only.

Videos are bundled in mobile builds. In a browser, the first load needs internet; later availability depends on your browser cache.

Find your next program

Use Library to preview free programs, adopt a weekly plan or edit your own routines. Explore alternatives for your equipment and keep previous records when changing your plan.

See your own progress

Weight logging and local progress photos are optional. Charts use saved measurements; muscle sets and strength estimates use your actual workout records. Optional community comparisons need at least 30 contributors. DailyFit does not invent intake, calories or progress.
